Compare your trailer's GVWR to your vehicle's towing capacity.

Understanding the relationship between your vehicle's towing capacity and your trailer's Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) is the most important step for safe towing. Your vehicle's capacity must be *higher* than the trailer's total loaded weight to ensure proper handling, braking, and to prevent damage to your vehicle.

How is Towing Capacity Calculated?

A vehicle's Max Tow Rating is not just about engine power. It's a complex calculation set by the manufacturer that includes the engine, transmission, axle ratio, frame strength, and braking system. The official rating (found on your door sticker or in the manual) represents the *absolute maximum* weight the vehicle can pull under ideal conditions, assuming only a 150-lb driver and no other cargo. This is why we recommend an 80% "safe" capacity for a real-world buffer.
